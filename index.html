Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 18, 2026
1. Who We Are

WrkUp ("we", "our", "us") is a hyperlocal gig marketplace that connects verified skilled workers (Partners) with people and businesses (Clients) across India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the WrkUp mobile app or website.

By using WrkUp, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.
2. Information We Collect
2.1 Information you provide directly
  • Full name, phone number, and email address during registration
  • Profile photo uploaded by you
  • Aadhaar number (stored only as masked last 4 digits after verification)
  • Skill certificates and qualification documents uploaded by Partners
  • Job descriptions, location, and requirements posted by Clients
  • Bank account or UPI details added for withdrawals (Partners only)
  • Ratings and written feedback submitted after a job
  • Messages sent through our in-app chat feature
2.2 Information collected automatically
  • GPS location — collected only when the app is in use or a Partner is marked online. Background location is used only during active jobs so Clients can track their Partner.
  • Device identifiers (model, OS version) for push notification delivery
  • App usage data (screens viewed, features used) for product improvement
  • IP address and approximate location for fraud prevention
3. How We Use Your Information
  • To create and manage your account and profile
  • To match Partners with nearby job postings using GPS radius matching
  • To verify Partner identity through Aadhaar OTP (we do not store your full Aadhaar number)
  • To process payments and transfer earnings to Partner wallets
  • To send push notifications for new jobs, payment confirmations, and updates
  • To enable in-app chat without revealing personal phone numbers
  • To resolve disputes and provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • To improve features based on aggregated, anonymised usage data
4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Between users: When a job is accepted, the Client's job location and the Partner's name, photo, rating, and skills are shared to complete the job.
  • Razorpay: Payment processing. Your payment details are handled directly by Razorpay under their privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication (OTP), push notifications, and file storage.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data to law enforcement if required by Indian law.
5. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, personal data is deleted within 30 days, except financial records which are retained for 7 years under Indian tax law.

6. Your Rights
  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update details via the app's Edit Profile screen
  • Deletion: Request account deletion by emailing wrkup.app@gmail.com
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Disable push notifications in app or device settings at any time
7. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit. Passwords are never stored — we use Firebase phone OTP. Aadhaar numbers are never stored in full. File uploads are stored in Firebase Storage with access controls.

If you suspect unauthorised access, contact us immediately at wrkup.app@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

WrkUp is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Accounts found to belong to minors will be deleted immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify registered users of material changes via push notification or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
